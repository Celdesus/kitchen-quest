# Kitchen Quest – Mock Job Library & Points System (v5)

> Note: this app is not the kitchen's food safety record. Any temperature, cleaning and sanitising records the business is required to keep stay in its existing food safety system. The temp checks below are ordinary game jobs only.

## Ground rules
- Only managers create, edit or retire jobs and set point values. Staff cannot add or suggest jobs in the app.
- Points pay out only after verification (self-tick, photo or manager sign-off, depending on the job).
- Photos show the bench, equipment or product only, never people.

## Point scale
- 5 pts: quick, under 10 min
- 10 pts: standard, 10–30 min
- 20 pts: big, 30–60 min
- 30 pts: deep clean, 60+ min
- +50% "grim job" multiplier (drains, bins, behind equipment, grease trap area)
- Quick checks (temp checks, sanitiser bucket): flat 3 pts each, fixed number per shift

Verification key: T = self-tick, P = in-app photo (equipment/bench only), M = manager sign-off

## Shift handover
Written by the closing or outgoing shift lead, confirmed by the incoming lead. 10 pts for writing it, 5 pts for reading and confirming it.
Covers:
- Prep done and outstanding, with dates on anything carried over
- Equipment faults and what's been reported
- Open customer issues (complaints, allergen follow-ups)
- Deliveries due or problems with today's deliveries
- Staffing gaps or changes for the next shift

## Job rotation
- Managers assign high-point and grim jobs on a rotation across sections and people, so the same crew (often dish) aren't stuck with the low-value grind and the big jobs aren't always grabbed by the same few people.
- The app shows each person's mix of jobs over the last 4 weeks so managers can spot imbalance.
- Dish crew get first offer on rotated high-point jobs when their recent average is below the team's.

## Outlets and fair points
The app spans multiple outlets, each with its own leaderboard plus one group-wide leaderboard.

**The one-line version for staff:**
> "Your outlet board ranks your points per hour worked. The group board ranks how far above your own outlet's average you are, so every kitchen has a fair shot."

**How it works**
- **One group job library and point scale.** The same job is worth the same points at every outlet. Outlet managers pick which jobs their outlet uses and can add local jobs, but only on the group scale.
- **Hours come from the admin portal.** Managers enter each person's hours worked for the week (Monday to Sunday) before the weekly results are calculated.
- **Outlet board** ranks on **points per hour worked**. Points per hour is fair between full-timers, part-timers and casuals, and between short and long shifts.
- **Group board** ranks on a **Quest Score**: (your points per hour ÷ your outlet's average points per hour that week) × 100. A score of 100 means you matched your outlet's average.
- **Minimum hours to be ranked:** an example 15 hours in the week. Anyone under it still earns points and badges but isn't ranked that week.
- **Small outlets (behind the scenes, optional):** if an outlet has fewer than an example 5 ranked staff in a week, its average is blended with the group average so one person's big week doesn't skew everyone's score. Staff don't need to know this detail.
- **Rush bonus** points are left out of the outlet average, so rushes don't skew the Quest Score.
- **Rookie tier:** staff in their first 6 weeks (example) have their own board at each outlet and across the group, using the same rules.
- Section standings run within each outlet, and outlet-vs-outlet standings use each outlet's average Quest Score.

**Is the Quest Score still needed?** Yes, for the group board only. Points per hour fixes differences in hours and shift length, but it doesn't fix differences in busyness: a busy flagship simply has more jobs per hour than a quiet café, so on raw points per hour the busiest outlet would win the group almost every week (see the example below). The outlet boards don't need it, so most staff only ever see points per hour. If Ivan wants it even simpler, the group board could show "% above your outlet's average" (for example +35%) instead of a score out of 100; the maths is the same.

**What this tests**
- On raw points per hour, Sam (6.9) would win the group and Jess (3.8) would sit near the bottom, purely because the café is quieter. With the Quest Score, Jess wins because she was furthest above her own outlet's average.
- Leo works only 20 hours but ranks 5th in the group, so part-timers can compete.
- Mia at the small kiosk is compared with a blended average, so her score doesn't swing wildly.
- Raf had a strong rate (4.8 per hour) but only 10 hours, so he isn't ranked. A couple of big shifts can't top the board.
- Each outlet crowns its own winner (Sam, Tom, Jess, Mia), and Jess is Group Champion.

## Weekly crown
- Weeks run Monday to Sunday. Managers enter hours, the app auto-calculates the winners after Sunday's close, and Ivan signs them off before they're announced.
- Each week has a crowned winner at each outlet (top points per hour) and a **Group Champion** (top Quest Score).
- Ties go to the person with more verified jobs that week, then to more deep cleans.
- The winner gets a crown on their profile for the week and is shown in the group feed. Past winners are kept in a Hall of Fame.

## Streaks
- A streak is **consecutive weeks finishing in the top 3**, with one streak for the outlet board and one for the group board.
- Finishing outside the top 3 while ranked ends the streak.
- A week **below the minimum hours or on approved leave pauses the streak**. It neither grows nor ends, and picks up again the next ranked week.
- Streak milestones: 2, 4, 8 and 12 weeks, each with a badge (for example Hot Streak, On Fire, Unstoppable, Legend).

## Badges (free)
- Temp Champ: 30 on-time temp checks
- Deep Diver: 5 verified deep cleans
- Fryer Whisperer: 10 fryer filters or boil-outs
- Rush Hero: on shift for 5 rush bonuses with close-down passed
- Spotter: reported 5 equipment faults that were confirmed
- Mentor: buddied a new starter through their first week (manager awarded)
- No Shortcuts: a month with no points reversed

## Team goals (weekly)
- 95% of checklists verified on time
- Every rostered deep clean done
- Every handover written and confirmed
Set per outlet. Hitting a goal unlocks a shared reward the manager chooses; hitting 85% unlocks a smaller one.

## Rush bonus
Flat 10 pts each for the crew on a manager-flagged slammed service, paid only if close-down passes.

## Weekly rewards (suggestions only)
Rewards are TBC. Each week the manager types in the reward as free text (per outlet and for the Group Champion). Ideas to get started:
- Weekly crown winner: golden spatula trophy, first pick of roster swap or preferred station
- Top 3: name on the board and a shout-out in the group feed
- Rookie winner: separate shout-out and a small treat
- Section win: manager shouts coffees or a team snack

**Check before using:**
- *Early finish*: check against the relevant award and Fair Work rules (pay, rostered hours, breaks) before offering it. A safer swap is first pick of a preferred shift or station.
- *Knock-off drink*: check against responsible service of alcohol rules, your liquor licence and workplace policy. Suggested non-alcohol options: a specialty coffee, a smoothie or soft drink, a staff meal upgrade or a dessert from pastry.

## Safeguards against gaming
- P and M jobs stay pending until approved.
- Daily cap on repeatable quick jobs (e.g. restock max 3 per shift).
- Random manager spot checks on self-ticked jobs. A failed check reverses that job's points and puts it back on the list.
- Photos are taken in-app, timestamped and of equipment or benches only. No gallery uploads or reused photos.
- Jobs can only be claimed while rostered on and inside the job's time window.
- No one verifies their own job, and managers earn no points on jobs they sign off.
- Quick checks are flat points and a fixed number per shift, so they can't be farmed. Reporting an equipment fault is never penalised.
- Ticking a job that wasn't done reverses its points; repeat cases go to the manager.
- Ranking on points per hour (outlet) and Quest Score (group), with a minimum hours rule, plus rotating big jobs, stops a few people or one busy outlet owning the leaderboard.
- A shared group point scale stops outlets inflating their local job values.
- Staff who work at more than one outlet have their hours entered per outlet, count toward each outlet's board for the hours worked there, and appear once on the group board with a Quest Score weighted by their hours at each outlet.
- Hours entered by managers lock once Ivan signs off the week, and every change is logged. Managers can't enter or edit their own hours. Hours should match the roster or timesheets, and a big mismatch is flagged for Ivan.
- Because points per hour is a rate, padding hours lowers your rate and leaving hours off raises it, so the hours check matters most for people near the minimum.
- Team goals weigh as much as individual points to keep it cooperative.
- Monthly manager review of point values to rebalance jobs that are always grabbed or always avoided.
