# Kitchen Quest – Mock Job Library & Points System (v1)
**EXAMPLE DATA ONLY. Not Ivan's real lists. Replace with real checklists before launch.**

## Rules that sit above the points
- Only managers create, edit or retire jobs and set point values. Staff cannot add or suggest jobs in the app.
- Food safety jobs are mandatory and never skipped for points. They pay a small, steady amount so nobody rushes them.
- A missed or falsified food safety record cancels that person's bonus points for the shift (base points stay).
- Points are paid only once a job is verified (self-tick, photo, or manager sign-off depending on the job).

## Point scale
- 5 pts: quick, under 10 min
- 10 pts: standard, 10–30 min
- 20 pts: big, 30–60 min
- 30 pts: deep clean, 60+ min
- +50% "grim job" multiplier on jobs nobody wants (drains, grease trap area, bins)
- Food safety logs: flat 3 pts each, compulsory

Verification key: T = self-tick, P = photo proof, M = manager sign-off

## Streaks
- Clean Close streak (section): consecutive shifts passing manager close-down check.
- Log Keeper streak (person): every food safety log on time for 7 shifts.
- Streak freeze: one per month, earned, so a day off or a sick day doesn't kill a streak.

## Badges (free)
- Temp Champ: 30 on-time temperature logs
- Deep Diver: 5 deep cleans verified
- Fryer Whisperer: 10 fryer filters/boil-outs
- Rush Hero: on shift for 5 rush bonuses with close-down passed
- Mentor: buddied a new starter through their first week (manager awarded)
- No Shortcuts: a month with zero points reversed

## Team goals (weekly)
- 95% of checklists verified on time
- Zero missed food safety logs
- Every deep clean on the roster done
Unlock: a shared reward (below). Partial credit at 85% for a smaller one.

## Rush bonus
Flat 10 pts each for the crew on a manager-flagged slammed service, paid only if close-down passes that night.

## Cheap weekly rewards
- Choose staff meal / family meal theme
- Manager shouts coffees for the winning section
- First pick of roster swap or preferred station
- Leave 15 minutes early (with close-down done)
- Free drink after shift
- "Golden spatula" trophy for the week's top person
- Name on the board / shout-out in the group chat
- Monthly: small voucher ($20–30) for top scorer and for most improved

## Safeguards against gaming
- Points only paid after verification; P and M jobs stay pending until approved.
- Daily cap on repeatable quick jobs (e.g. restock max 3 per shift).
- Random manager spot checks on self-ticked jobs; a failed check reverses points and adds the job back.
- Photos are taken in-app with timestamp (no gallery uploads), one photo can't be reused.
- Jobs can only be claimed while rostered on and within the job's time window.
- No one can verify their own job; managers can't earn points on jobs they sign off.
- Reward both top scorer and most improved so a few people can't own the leaderboard.
- Team goals weigh as much as individual points to keep it cooperative.
- Food safety records count toward team goals but can't be farmed (flat 3 pts, fixed number per shift).
- Monthly manager review of point values: rebalance jobs that are always claimed or always avoided.
