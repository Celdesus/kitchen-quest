# Kitchen Quest – Mock Job Library & Points System (v7)

> Note: this app is not the kitchen's food safety record. Any temperature, cleaning and sanitising records the business is required to keep stay in its existing food safety system. The temp checks below are ordinary game jobs only.

## Ground rules
- Only managers create, edit or retire jobs and set point values. Staff cannot add or suggest jobs in the app.
- Points pay out only after verification (self-tick, photo or manager sign-off, depending on the job).
- Photos show the bench, equipment or product only, never people.

## Point scale
- 5 pts: quick, under 10 min
- 10 pts: standard, 10–30 min
- 20 pts: big, 30–60 min
- 30 pts: deep clean, 60+ min
- +50% "grim job" multiplier (drains, bins, behind equipment, grease trap area)
- Quick checks (temp checks, sanitiser bucket): flat 3 pts each, fixed number per shift

Verification key: T = self-tick, P = in-app photo (equipment/bench only), M = manager sign-off

## Shift handover
Written by the closing or outgoing shift lead, confirmed by the incoming lead. 10 pts for writing it, 5 pts for reading and confirming it.
Covers:
- Prep done and outstanding, with dates on anything carried over
- Equipment faults and what's been reported
- Open customer issues (complaints, allergen follow-ups)
- Deliveries due or problems with today's deliveries
- Staffing gaps or changes for the next shift

## Job rotation
- Managers assign high-point and grim jobs on a rotation across sections and people, so the same crew (often dish) aren't stuck with the low-value grind and the big jobs aren't always grabbed by the same few people.
- The app shows each person's mix of jobs over the last 4 weeks so managers can spot imbalance.
- Dish crew get first offer on rotated high-point jobs when their recent average is below the team's.

## Outlets and fair points
The app spans multiple outlets, each with its own leaderboard plus one group-wide leaderboard.

**The one-line version for staff:**
> "Every board ranks your points per hour worked, at your outlet and across the group."

**How it works**
- **One group job library and point scale.** The same job is worth the same points at every outlet. Outlet managers pick which jobs their outlet uses and can add local jobs, but only on the group scale.
- **Hours come from the admin portal.** Ivan (the owner) enters each person's hours worked for the week (Monday to Sunday) before the weekly results are calculated. Managers can't enter or edit hours.
- **Outlet board and group board** both rank on **points per hour worked**. That's fair between full-timers, part-timers and casuals, and between short and long shifts.
- **Minimum hours to be ranked:** 10 hours in the week. Anyone under it still earns points and badges but isn't ranked that week.
- **Everyone is on the boards.** There's no opt-out: every staff member, lead and manager who meets the minimum hours is ranked.
- **Leads and managers compete too**, on the same points-per-hour rules. They never verify their own jobs, and they earn no points for jobs they sign off for others. Their own jobs are verified by another manager or by Ivan.
- **Rookie tier:** staff in their first 6 weeks (example) have their own board at each outlet and across the group, using the same rules.
- Section standings run within each outlet, and outlet-vs-outlet standings use each outlet's average points per hour.
- Every outlet still crowns its own weekly winner, so each kitchen has a champion even if busier outlets tend to lead the group board.

## Weekly crown
- Weeks run Monday to Sunday. Ivan enters hours, the app auto-calculates the winners after Sunday's close, and Ivan signs them off before they're announced.
- Each week has a crowned winner at each outlet (top points per hour) and a **Group Champion** (top points per hour across the group).
- Ties go to the person with more verified jobs that week, then to more deep cleans.
- The winner gets a crown on their profile for the week and is shown in the group feed. Past winners are kept in a Hall of Fame.

## Streaks
- A streak is **consecutive weeks finishing in the top 3**, with one streak for the outlet board and one for the group board.
- Finishing outside the top 3 while ranked ends the streak.
- A week **below the minimum hours or on approved leave pauses the streak**. It neither grows nor ends, and picks up again the next ranked week.
- Streak milestones: 2, 4, 8 and 12 weeks, each with a badge (for example Hot Streak, On Fire, Unstoppable, Legend).

## Seasons
- A season runs **10–12 weeks**. Ivan sets each season's length within that range when he creates it, and it always starts on a Monday and ends on a Sunday.
- The length is chosen with staff turnover in mind: long enough for a real race, short enough that most staff are around for a full season.
- **Season standing = total season points ÷ total season hours**, so it stays points per hour like the weekly boards.
- To be in the running for Season Champion, someone must be ranked (10+ hours) in at least **60% of the season's weeks** (rounded up: 6 of 10, 7 of 11 or 8 of 12 weeks). Example number.
- **Turnover:** staff who start partway through a season compete on the rookie board and for Rookie of the Season, then join the main race next season. Staff who leave keep their Hall of Fame entries.
- **Season Champions:** one per outlet and one Group Season Champion. The app calculates them after the final Sunday and Ivan signs them off, the same as the weekly crown.
- **What resets:** season points, season boards and season standings.
- **What carries over:** badges, Hall of Fame entries, lifetime points and top-3 streaks (a streak is weekly, so it rolls on from one season to the next).
- Suggested end-of-season extras (manager free-text reward): Season Champion title on profile for the next season, plus a Rookie of the Season.

### Season 1 launch week: double points
- In week 1 of Season 1, every job is worth **double points** (a 10-point job pays 20).
- How it works with points per hour: because everyone's points double, the **weekly ranking order doesn't change**. It's there to make the launch feel big and to fast-track early badges and milestones.
- For the **season standing**, launch week counts at **normal (single) value**. Otherwise people rostered for lots of hours in week 1 would get a lasting head start that's nothing to do with how well they work.
- Rule for staff: "Launch week: every job pays double. Your weekly rank and badges use the doubled points; your season rank doesn't."
- Optional variant (if Ivan wants double points to change behaviour): double points only on deep cleans and grim jobs for launch week, to get the worst jobs done early. This does change rankings, so it would count at double in the season too.

## Peer shout-outs
- Each person can give **one shout-out per week** to a colleague at any outlet, with a short reason (for example "covered the dish pit in the rush"). Managers can give one too.
- **Recognition only:** shout-outs show on the receiver's profile and in the group feed and count toward badges. They add **no points** and don't change anyone's points per hour, so rankings stay plain points per hour.
- Unused shout-outs don't roll over.
- **Safeguards:** no shout-outs to yourself; the reason is required and visible to the receiver and managers; managers can remove one that breaks the rules; if the same two people swap shout-outs 3 weeks in a row, it's flagged to the manager (still allowed, just checked).
- Badge: **Team Player** for receiving 10 shout-outs, and **Hype Crew** for giving a shout-out 8 weeks in a row.

## Outlet vs outlet challenges
- Only Ivan (the owner) sets challenges, each month (or fortnight), between two outlets or all four. Each challenge runs Monday to Sunday over 1–2 weeks.
- Challenges are measured on **outlet averages or completion rates**, never totals, so a small outlet can beat a big one. Examples:
  - Highest outlet average points per hour for the challenge period
  - Most deep cleans completed on time, as a % of those rostered
  - Highest % of handovers written and confirmed
  - Fewest jobs reversed on spot checks
- **Prize:** the winning outlet gets a challenge trophy in the app, a Hall of Fame entry and a team reward Ivan types in.
- **Points:** challenges don't add personal points, so they don't change anyone's points per hour.
- Badge: **Outlet Pride** for being on a winning challenge team 3 times.

## Hall of Fame
A permanent page across all outlets showing:
- Every weekly crown (each outlet plus Group Champion)
- Every Season Champion (each outlet plus Group) and Rookie of the Season
- Outlet challenge winners
- Longest top-3 streak, at each outlet and across the group
- Best single-week points per hour (only weeks with 10+ hours count)
- Most shout-outs received in a season
Entries are added only after Ivan signs off that week or season.

## Badges (free)
- Temp Champ: 30 on-time temp checks
- Deep Diver: 5 verified deep cleans
- Fryer Whisperer: 10 fryer filters or boil-outs
- Rush Hero: on shift for 5 rush bonuses with close-down passed
- Spotter: reported 5 equipment faults that were confirmed
- Mentor: buddied a new starter through their first week (manager awarded)
- No Shortcuts: a month with no points reversed

## Team goals (weekly)
- 95% of checklists verified on time
- Every rostered deep clean done
- Every handover written and confirmed
Set per outlet. Hitting a goal unlocks a shared reward the manager chooses; hitting 85% unlocks a smaller one.

## Rush bonus
Flat 10 pts each for the crew on a manager-flagged slammed service, paid only if close-down passes.

## Safeguards against gaming
- P and M jobs stay pending until approved.
- Daily cap on repeatable quick jobs (e.g. restock max 3 per shift).
- Random manager spot checks on self-ticked jobs. A failed check reverses that job's points and puts it back on the list.
- Photos are taken in-app, timestamped and of equipment or benches only. No gallery uploads or reused photos.
- Jobs can only be claimed while rostered on and inside the job's time window.
- No one verifies their own job, and leads and managers earn no points on jobs they sign off for others.
- Quick checks are flat points and a fixed number per shift, so they can't be farmed. Reporting an equipment fault is never penalised.
- Ticking a job that wasn't done reverses its points; repeat cases go to the manager.
- Ranking on points per hour with a minimum hours rule, plus rotating big jobs, stops a few people or one busy outlet owning the leaderboard.
- A shared group point scale stops outlets inflating their local job values.
- Staff who work at more than one outlet have their hours entered per outlet, count toward each outlet's board for the hours worked there, and appear once on the group board using their total points ÷ total hours.
- Only Ivan enters hours. They lock once he signs off the week, and every change is logged. Hours should match the roster or timesheets, and the app flags a big mismatch (for example hours entered for someone with no verified jobs that week).
- Because points per hour is a rate, padding hours lowers your rate and leaving hours off raises it, so the hours check matters most for people near the minimum.
- Team goals weigh as much as individual points to keep it cooperative.
- Monthly manager review of point values to rebalance jobs that are always grabbed or always avoided.

## Pilot and rollout plan
Order: **Boughshed Kitchen** first, then **Monkey Bar Kitchen**, then **Monkey Bar** and **Boughshed Bar**. Timings below are suggestions.

### Stage 1: Boughshed Kitchen pilot (3 weeks)
**Week 0 (setup, before the pilot starts)**
- Boughshed Kitchen managers load the outlet's real jobs from the group job library and set any local jobs on the group scale.
- Add all Boughshed Kitchen staff and roles. Ivan practises entering hours; managers practise verifying jobs and doing spot checks.
- A 10-minute staff briefing using the one-liners: points per hour, the weekly crown, streaks and shout-outs.

**Week 1: learn**
- Jobs, points and verification go live. The leaderboard is visible but there's no crown yet.
- No double points; save that for the Season 1 group launch.
- Managers note any jobs that are confusing, always skipped, or too slow to verify.

**Week 2: compete**
- The weekly crown and peer shout-outs go live. Ivan signs off the first Boughshed Kitchen winner.
- Managers fix the point values flagged in week 1.

**Week 3: full rules**
- Top-3 streaks start counting, and the rookie tier is on if there are new starters.
- Ivan runs a mini challenge against Boughshed Kitchen's own week 2 (for example "beat last week's handover %") to test the challenge flow.

**Go / no-go to move on to Monkey Bar Kitchen (example targets)**
- 80%+ of jobs verified in week 3
- Under 5% of jobs reversed on spot checks
- Manager sign-off time under 30 minutes a week
- Most Boughshed Kitchen staff answer "yes" to keeping it

### Stage 2: Monkey Bar Kitchen (2–3 weeks)
- Fix the issues found at Boughshed Kitchen first.
- Run the same setup and briefing. Boughshed Kitchen's head chef or a Boughshed Kitchen regular can help with the briefing.
- Monkey Bar Kitchen can move through the stages faster (for example the crown from week 1) if setup goes smoothly.
- First test of the group features: the group board with two outlets, the first **Boughshed Kitchen vs Monkey Bar Kitchen** challenge (judged on averages or completion rates), and shout-outs across outlets.
- Boughshed Kitchen keeps running as normal; its pilot weeks count for badges and the Hall of Fame but not for Season 1.
- Check the same go / no-go targets at Monkey Bar Kitchen, and compare the two outlets' points per hour to see whether busier outlets lead the group board.

### Stage 3: Monkey Bar and Boughshed Bar
- Onboard them together or one after the other, using the same setup and briefing.
- These are bars, so before they join, their managers need bar jobs added to the group library on the same point scale (for example glass washer clean, beer line clean, bar fridge restock, ice well clean and empty, back-bar wipe-down, closing stock count). The current library is kitchen jobs only.
- Use 1–2 weeks of learning time, then join the group board.

### Season 1 launch
- Once all four outlets are live, Season 1 starts on a Monday at every outlet, with a double-points launch week.
- Season boards start from zero for everyone, so the pilot outlets don't get a head start.

### What to measure at every stage
- % of jobs completed and verified each week
- % of jobs reversed on spot checks (to spot gaming)
- Whether Ivan's hours entry and sign-off happen on time, and how many minutes a week they take (plus managers' time on job verification)
- Jobs that are never claimed or always grabbed
- Shout-outs given per person per week
- A 3-question staff survey at the end of each stage: Is it fair? Is it easy? Do you want to keep it?
