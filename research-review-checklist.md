# Kitchen Quest: research review checklist (from Grok Bot, 2 Nov 2026)

Key finding: if the kitchen is "category one" under FSANZ Standard 3.2.2A (most restaurants making ready-to-eat food from raw), cleaning/sanitising and temperature records are legal evidence: made daily, dated (timed where relevant), kept >= 3 months, available on site to an officer whenever open. Electronic records and photos are allowed. Sanitising records should capture method: heat (water temp, contact time), chemical (what/how/when), dishwasher (manufacturer's sanitising setting). Ivan to confirm category with council EHO.
Sources: https://www.health.wa.gov.au/FSMT , https://www.foodauthority.nsw.gov.au/retail/standard-322a-food-safety-management-tools/showing-food-safe

## Must-have
1. Only managers add/edit jobs, points, rewards via admin portal; changes live; every change logged (who, when, old/new value).
2. Jobs carry station, shift (AM/PM/close), frequency, due time, points, high-risk flag, photo/reading required.
3. Compliance records can't be edited afterwards: date/time, staff, task, reading where relevant (sanitiser + method, water temp + contact time, dishwasher setting, fridge/food temps). Keep >= 3 months (prefer 12+). One-tap "show the inspector" view/export that works on site offline.
4. Failed/out-of-range check auto-creates a fix-it task and alerts a manager.
5. Digital shift handover: prep done + dates, equipment faults, open customer issues, deliveries due, staffing changes. Carries over until incoming lead confirms; stricter for the weekend gap.
6. Shared kitchen tablet first: quick PIN/tap-name login, glove-size buttons, high contrast, <= 3 taps per task, readable at 1 m, works offline then syncs.
7. Speed and reliability over features (main complaint about existing apps).
8. Points only after quality check (manager/lead sign-off or photo; spot checks on high-risk cleans); points reversible.
9. Reward done-properly-and-on-time, never speed.
10. Fair leaderboard: points per shift worked and/or team standings so casuals/part-timers aren't stuck at the bottom.
11. Rules shown in plain language; staff see why they got each point.
12. Simple English + icons; room for translated/pictured task cards.
13. Messaging: manager-to-all announcements/incentives with read receipts, plus 1:1.
14. Privacy: no location or audio, photos of work not people, staff told what's tracked (WA Surveillance Devices Act).
15. Food Safety Supervisor named in app, with certificate expiry and staff training records.

## Nice-to-have
Multiple ways to win (team goals, most improved, streaks, helped another station, peer shout-outs); tiered weekly rewards; staff vote on rewards; streaks that skip days off; shared prep list with quantities ("4L made"); Bluetooth probes; translations/read-aloud/how-to videos; trends dashboard; rookie mode/leaderboard; optional personal phone app.

## Avoid
Speed scoring or anything that shortens sanitiser contact time; points without quality checks; winner-takes-all or a single all-time shaming leaderboard; forced participation or using points for discipline (keep compliance record separate from the game); self-claiming high-point jobs with no rotation; relying on personal phones during service; editable/deletable or cloud-only records; location, audio, face photos; long forms and admin bloat; slang-heavy text-only screens.
